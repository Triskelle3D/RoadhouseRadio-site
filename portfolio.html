3D Printing, Micro-Electronics & Smart Home

Triskelle 3D

Interactive Brain Midi Controller

The making side of the practice: turning filament, microcontrollers, and sensors into things that print, move, react, and report back.

Triskelle 3D covers 3D printing, micro-electronics, and smart-home territory: printed parts and enclosures, microcontrollers wired into real builds, and Home Assistant installs that tie it all together.
